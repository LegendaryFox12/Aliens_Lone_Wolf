# Aliens_Lone_Wolf
A little terminal-based game I designed off of the movie Aliens. The first time I saw Aliens, I fell in love. The very next day
I rewatched it. By now, I've seen it more times than I can count. And I didn't stop there. Eventually, I saw every movie in the
franchise, including the show. I will never forget the times when I saw the movies with my dad, and I can't wait to see what
the franchise has to offer in the future. This game is a good mix of chance and strategy polished off with a campaign about a Marine
on a rescue mission. A 'Lone Wolf' if you will.
